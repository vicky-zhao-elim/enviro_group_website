School Gardening Efforts

By Firstname Lastname | Weekday Date Month Year

This term the Enviro Group has been working on the newly opened garden outside of our music/art room. You may have noticed some new garden plots there. The Enviro Group has been hard at work planting a variety of vegetables there.

One member says, "It's really fun. You get to plant things and watch them grow eventually." So far we have planted tomatoes, potatoes, cabbages, and rubbarb. We hope to be able to plant some more in the future. We are thinking of planting some trees eventually.
