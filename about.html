About

Hello! Welcome to the Elim Enviro Group website. We are dedicated to making Elim Christian College Mount Albert into a better, cleaner, and more beautiful school. Our team works hard to ensure that Elim students understand how to take care of our school.

But what exactly do we do? We have a focus on informing Elim students on how to dispose of rubbish to reduce litter around the school. We also take care of the school garden. We hope that our efforts preserve the beauty of our school and the wider environment. It is written in Genesis 1:26: " Then God said, "Let us make mankind in our image, in our likeness, so that they may rule over the fish in the sea and the birds in the sky, over the livestock and all the wild animals, and over all the creatures that move along the ground." " God chose us to rule over the Earth, so we should do our best to preserve and care for it.
